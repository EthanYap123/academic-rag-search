# Internship Programme FAQ

Northbridge Polytechnic, School of Computing. This FAQ covers the compulsory internship taken in the final year of every diploma.

## Who must do an internship?

Every full-time diploma student must complete one internship of 22 weeks before graduating. Students must have passed all Year 1 and Year 2 core modules and have a CGPA of at least 2.0 before they can be matched to a company.

## How are students matched to companies?

Companies post openings on the Internship Portal in Semester 1 of the final year. Students may apply for up to five openings at a time. A student who accepts an offer is withdrawn from all other applications and may not reject the offer afterwards, except on medical grounds approved by the Internship Office.

Students who are not matched by the end of Week 10 are assigned an opening by the Internship Office, taking their preferences into account where possible.

## Can I find my own internship?

Yes. A self-sourced internship must be approved by the Internship Office before it starts. The company must be a registered business, the work must be related to the diploma, and the student must be supervised by an employee with at least three years of relevant experience. Apply for approval at least four weeks before the planned start date.

## Is the internship paid?

Companies are encouraged, but not required, to pay an allowance. Most School of Computing interns receive a monthly allowance of between 800 and 1,200 dollars. The polytechnic does not top up allowances.

## How is the internship graded?

The internship is graded Pass or Fail and is worth 20 credits. The grade is based on:

- the company supervisor's evaluation (40 percent),
- a mid-point reflection report (20 percent),
- the final report and presentation (40 percent).

A student who fails the internship must repeat it in full, which normally delays graduation by one semester.

## What if something goes wrong during the internship?

Contact your polytechnic supervisor first. For problems with safety, harassment or unpaid allowances, also contact the Internship Office, which may arrange a transfer to another company. Students must not leave an internship without approval; doing so results in a Fail grade.
