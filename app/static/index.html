Ask the handbooks.

Questions are answered only from the indexed documents. Every claim links to the exact page and section it came from, and if the documents don't cover something, it says so instead of guessing.

Source passages for the selected answer appear here, with their document, page and section.

    How it works

    1. IngestPDFs are read page by page with pypdf; Markdown by heading. Running headers and page numbers are stripped, and section headings are tracked.
    2. ChunkText is split into sentence-aligned chunks of about 900 characters with a small overlap. Chunks never cross a page or section, so each one has an exact citation.
    3. Embed & storeEach chunk is embedded locally with all-MiniLM-L6-v2 (ONNX) and stored in ChromaDB with its title, page and section.
    4. RetrieveThe question is embedded and matched by cosine similarity, then re-ranked with a keyword bonus. Chunks below a relevance cut-off are discarded.
    5. GroundWith a Gemini key, the model sees only the numbered chunks and must cite one for every claim, and answers without valid citations are rejected. Without a key, the extractive answerer quotes the best sentences verbatim.
    6. CiteCitation chips link each claim to its passage, and the original file opens at that page.
